How to Use

Reservation Flow

Before Your Visit

  1. 1
    Choose your date and duration Select the date and duration on the booking page, then choose from the available start times.
  2. 2
    Submit a pending booking request Enter your details and submit your request. Your booking is confirmed only after we send you a confirmation.
  3. 3
    Receive the room number and passcode After your booking is confirmed, we will send you the room number and entry passcode for your visit.

    4. 🔒

    Please be aware of the auto-lock door

    The door locks automatically. If you step into the hallway without your passcode, you will be locked out. Always confirm your passcode on your phone before stepping outside, even briefly — for example, when bringing in luggage.

  4. 4
    Review the entry instructions Please check how to locate the key, unlock the door, and turn on the lights before your visit.
    📄 Key location guide (PDF)
    📄 Lock instructions (PDF)
    📄 Switch instructions (PDF)
  5. 5
    Pay the fee (prepayment) Cash: Write your booking name and amount on the envelope, then place it in the payment box inside the room. PayPay: Scan the QR code in the room to pay. (PayPay is a Japanese mobile payment app; a Japanese phone number is required to register.) Credit card: Online payment by credit card is also available. Contact us for details.
  6. 6
    Start your session Once you are ready, feel free to use the studio freely.
    📄 Tatami mat instructions (PDF) — traditional Japanese straw flooring; please remove shoes before stepping on it
    📄 Hue lighting instructions (PDF)

After Your Visit

  1. 7
    Return items to their original place Please return all equipment and props to where you found them.
  2. 8
    Quick clean-up Please sweep the floor with the vacuum cleaner or floor wiper provided.
  3. 9
    Turn off A/C and lights Turn off all lights and air conditioning before leaving.
  4. 10
    Lock up and return the key Lock the door, return the key to the key box outside, and set the dial to 0000.

Ways to Use the Studio

Usage Scenes

Regular practice
From floor work to partial and full suspension: practice that is hard to do at home, repeating new ties, checking form, and reviewing with video or photos.

Show and act rehearsal
Full run-throughs before a performance, suspension sequences, and positioning checks with the large mirror, plus floor work on the tatami and the transition from suspension to the floor.

Small workshops
Lessons, private coaching and skill sharing among rope practitioners are welcome. The studio itself does not provide rope instruction. Please contact us in advance if you plan to hold a workshop.

Creative rope shoots
A fully private room lets you focus on rope and body-expression work. The lighting is set up for good results even with a smartphone, and you are welcome to bring your own camera and lighting. Commercial and paid creative projects are allowed with the consent of everyone involved.

Adults 18+ only. Sexual acts and sexual services are prohibited. Please read the safety rules before booking.

Safety Rules & Studio Details

Please Read Before Booking

  • All users and accompanying guests must be 18 or older.
  • Sexual acts and sexual services are prohibited.
  • Rope suspension must only be performed by experienced users.
  • Suspension use requires at least three people.
  • Suspension from the neck is strictly prohibited.
  • Rope activity after consuming alcohol is prohibited.
  • The studio does not provide rope instruction or technical lessons.
  • Users are responsible for their own safety and consent.
  • Follow all instructions regarding equipment and studio use.

Food and drinks are permitted only around the low table and school desk area. Eating and drinking are prohibited in the tatami area and near the suspension frame. Please take all trash with you.

Alcohol may be brought in, but parties and drinking gatherings are not permitted. Rope activities after drinking alcohol are strictly prohibited.

SNB MEMBER

SNB MEMBER costs ¥500 per month (tax included) and renews automatically by credit card, based on your join date (valid for one month from the day you join). SNB MEMBER is a perk for regular and long-stay guests: from 3 hours the member rate is lower than the general rate, and at 4 hours the difference equals the monthly fee. For 1–2 hours the rates are the same. Details and sign-up are on the membership page (Japanese).

Shower

Shower: SNB MEMBER-only option from Dec 1, 2026 — ¥1,000 per booking (not per person), usable only within your booked time, for the number of people using the studio that day. Apply at least 1 week before your visit; availability is confirmed by our staff.

Pricing & Equipment Notes

The fee is charged per room, not per person. Basic studio equipment is included. For rope photography and suspension use, a booking of 3 hours or longer is recommended.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

I'm a first-time guest. Can I still use the studio?
Yes. The studio is fully private with unmanned entry, and entry instructions are sent after your booking is confirmed. The studio provides the space and equipment only; users take responsibility for their own practice, safety and consent.
Can I use the studio for rope practice and photography?
Yes. Rope practice, rehearsal and rope photography are welcome. Sexual acts and sexual services are strictly prohibited, and suspension should only be performed by experienced users with at least three people present.
Can I shoot with a smartphone?
Yes. The lighting is set up so you can get good results even with a smartphone.
Is commercial use allowed?
Yes. Commercial shoots and paid creative projects are allowed, provided all performers consent and applicable laws and platform rules are followed.
What payment methods are available?
Cash, PayPay (on-site), and online credit card payment are available.
Is there parking?
There is no dedicated parking. Please use a nearby coin-operated parking lot.
